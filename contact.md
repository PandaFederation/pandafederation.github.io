# Contact

Panda Federation is a small workshop in Arizona that publishes public-data tools for AI agents. Email is the fastest way to reach us. We do not publish a phone number or a street address.

Email: [support@pandafederation.com](mailto:support@pandafederation.com)

We reply by email within 1 business day (Monday–Friday, Arizona time, not US holidays).

Please include which product or listing you mean (for example the Apify Actor name, the MCP server, the Quiet File Team kit, a Fiverr or Upwork order, or a shop order) and any order or run id the platform gave you.

This site does not take card payments. How you buy, and who handles billing, depends on the product:

- **Apify Actors** and the Quiet File Team setup kit on the Apify Store are billed by Apify. Billing, invoices, and payment questions start with Apify, and [Apify's terms](https://docs.apify.com/legal/terms-of-service) apply to those purchases.
- **MCP server** installs from PyPI are free. When a server calls our Apify Actors, usage is billed to your own Apify account at the list rates on the Store page (also summarized on [pricing.md](pricing.md)). The Store page is authoritative.
- **Quiet File Team Setup Kit** is also sold as a paid skill on Agensi; Agensi's listing and terms apply to that purchase.
- **Fiverr, Upwork, and Agensi** services are billed on those platforms, under those platforms' terms. Use that platform's order messages for work already in progress there.
- **Hardware**, when it is in stock, is sold separately at [https://shop.pandafederation.com/](https://shop.pandafederation.com/). Use this same support address for shop questions once that catalog has items.

GitHub and X remain public channels, but email is the right path for support. [GitHub](https://github.com/pandadad0214) · [Follow on X: @PandaFedHQ](https://x.com/PandaFedHQ)
