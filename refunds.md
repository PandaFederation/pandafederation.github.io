# Refunds

Who issued the charge is who can refund it. We do not collect card payments on pandafederation.com, so we cannot refund an Apify, Agensi, Fiverr, or Upwork charge ourselves.

- **Apify Actors** and the Quiet File Team setup kit bought on the Apify Store: Apify's terms and billing rules apply, including whatever refund process Apify offers. If a run failed because of a bug in our Actor, email [support@pandafederation.com](mailto:support@pandafederation.com) with the Actor name and the run id and we will help you work it out, including with Apify when that is the right path.
- **Agensi skill** purchases: Agensi's terms and refund rules apply.
- **Fiverr, Upwork,** and other freelance-platform orders: that platform's order and dispute process applies.
- **MCP server** installs are free. Apify usage billed to your token follows Apify's billing rules.
- **Hardware** sold on [https://shop.pandafederation.com/](https://shop.pandafederation.com/) will follow the return terms shown on that shop's product page, cart, and checkout. The shop is empty today, so those terms are not in use yet.
- If we ever sell a digital product directly (for example a kit paid on this site), the refund terms for that product will be stated on its page before you pay. This page will be updated then. We are not selling a direct kit that way today.

We do not promise refunds for change of mind on completed platform purchases, and we do not promise results from public-data research (for example that a grant, bid, or lead list will win work).

To ask about a charge, email [support@pandafederation.com](mailto:support@pandafederation.com) with the platform, the order or run id, and a short description.
