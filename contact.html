Contact

Panda Federation is a small workshop in Arizona that publishes public-data tools for AI agents. Email is the fastest way to reach us. We do not publish a phone number or a street address.

Email: support@pandafederation.com

We reply by email within 1 business day (Monday–Friday, Arizona time, not US holidays).

Contact form

You can also use this form. It asks for your name, your email address, a topic, an optional order or run id, and your message. It sends your message to support@pandafederation.com, and we reply to the email address you enter. We do not send an automatic copy to you. The form needs JavaScript; if it does not work for you, email us instead.

This site does not take card payments. How you buy, and who handles billing, depends on the product:

  • Apify Actors and the Quiet File Team setup kit on the Apify Store are billed by Apify. Billing, invoices, and payment questions start with Apify, and Apify's terms apply to those purchases.
  • MCP server installs from PyPI are free. When a server calls our Apify Actors, usage is billed to your own Apify account at the list rates on the Store page (also summarized on pricing.md). The Store page is authoritative.
  • Quiet File Team Setup Kit is also sold as a paid skill on Agensi; Agensi's listing and terms apply to that purchase.
  • Fiverr, Upwork, and Agensi services are billed on those platforms, under those platforms' terms. Use that platform's order messages for work already in progress there.
  • Hardware, when it is in stock, is sold separately at https://shop.pandafederation.com/. Use this same support address for shop questions once that catalog has items.
