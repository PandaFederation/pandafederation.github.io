### Steel Panda

### Manual Remake

Rewrite a confusing manual (PDF or photos) into a clear, printable English PDF. A free sample and paid remake editions are both on the Apify Store listing; the free skill is on Agensi.
[GitHub](https://github.com/pandadad0214/manual-remake) [Apify Store](https://apify.com/pandadad0214/manual-remake) [Agensi](https://agensi.io/skills/manual-remake-confusing-manual-to-clear-pdf) [DevHunt](https://devhunt.org/tool/manual-remake)

#### Switch editions

- **Free sample** — Apify Store — start a run with Free sample ticked, or with files and links left empty; no key required. [Open](https://apify.com/pandadad0214/manual-remake)
- **Text-only remake** — Paid Apify edition. Upload your manual, add your key, and turn off picture reuse and cleaning. List rate is on the Apify Tools card and on pricing.md; the Store page is authoritative. [Open](https://apify.com/pandadad0214/manual-remake)
- **Standard remake** — Paid Apify edition with picture reuse and cleaning on. List rate is on the Apify Tools card and on pricing.md; the Store page is authoritative. [Open](https://apify.com/pandadad0214/manual-remake)
- **Agensi skill** — Free skill that points at the same tool. [Open](https://agensi.io/skills/manual-remake-confusing-manual-to-clear-pdf)

### Quiet File Team

Free Bash kit that lets several AI bots share one computer through files: task queue, pair notes, early exit. A paid setup-kit generator is on the Apify Store and Agensi; a free MCP companion explains and checks a team.
[Product page](quiet-file-team/) [GitHub](https://github.com/pandadad0214/quiet-file-team) [MCP product page](quiet-file-team-mcp/) [MCP repo](https://github.com/pandadad0214/quiet-file-team-mcp) [Apify Store](https://apify.com/pandadad0214/quiet-file-team-setup) [Agensi](https://agensi.io/skills/quiet-file-team-setup-kit)

#### Switch editions

- **Free kit** — product page on this site, plus the GitHub repo — clone and follow the README. [Open](quiet-file-team/)
- **Free MCP companion** — product page on this site, plus the GitHub repo. [Open](quiet-file-team-mcp/)
- **Setup kit generator** — Paid Apify Actor that builds a configured starter ZIP. List rate is on the Apify Tools card and on pricing.md; the Store page is authoritative. [Open](https://apify.com/pandadad0214/quiet-file-team-setup)
- **Agensi skill** — Paid skill for the same setup kit. Live price is on the Agensi listing. [Open](https://agensi.io/skills/quiet-file-team-setup-kit)
