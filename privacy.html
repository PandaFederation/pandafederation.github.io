Privacy

Panda Federation is a small workshop. We collect only what we need to run this website and to answer support mail, and we do not sell your information.

What we collect

  • If you email support@pandafederation.com, we receive your email address, the message, and any order or run details you include, so we can reply.
  • If you use the contact form, we receive your name, your email address, the topic you pick, the optional order or run id, and your message. The form is run by a small Cloudflare Worker: it stores the message in a Cloudflare D1 database and emails it to support@pandafederation.com through Resend, our email delivery service. We use it only to reply to you and keep it for up to 12 months. To limit abuse, the Worker also keeps a one-way hash of your IP address with the message, and short-lived rate-limit counters keyed by one-way hashes of your IP address and email address. We do not send an automatic copy of the form to you, and we do not add you to any mailing list.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. They may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to operate and secure the site. The pages also load Cloudflare Web Analytics, which helps us see aggregate traffic. It is not used to sell ads.
  • We do not have accounts, a shopping cart, or checkout on pandafederation.com. There is no customer payment information on this site. When you buy on Apify, Agensi, Fiverr, Upwork, or the hardware shop, that platform's privacy policy covers the information you give them.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to prevent fraud or abuse.

You can ask what information we have about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing support@pandafederation.com. We may need to keep some records (for example, a support thread about a paid order) for bookkeeping or legal reasons.

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update this page and the date at the bottom.
