About Panda Federation

A dad, a shared computer, and a team of bots

Panda Federation is one dad's workshop. I build public-data tools with a small team of AI bots that share one computer. One bot writes and tests code, another checks listings and links, another keeps the books and the calendar honest, and I decide what ships.

The bots don't run on trust. Every tool they build is tested against the official source it reads (Crossref, SAM.gov, the NPI Registry, state business filings, RDAP and others) before it goes public. Every listing is checked against the live store page. When a bot finds a page that hides instructions aimed at AI agents, it logs the page and doesn't follow them. That habit became a tool: the Hidden AI Instructions Scanner.

What we build

  • Public-data tools for AI agents. Apify Actors and MCP servers that pull from official sources: reference checks, federal grants and bids, official lead lists, domain and email security. (Apify tools · MCP servers)
  • Local-first tools for bot teams. Quiet File Team is the plain-file system our own bots use to share one computer: a task queue, short handoff notes, and an early exit so idle routines stop instead of spending tokens. Steel Panda and Watchdog Panda are the small shell tools that keep it running.
  • Free guides. The data broker removal guide grew out of our own opt-out work and became a page anyone can use.

How we work

  • Official sources only. If a tool can't name its source, it doesn't ship.
  • Free and paid editions always name each other. When a tool has a free version and a paid one, both pages say so and explain how to switch. Prices live on the store listing, never on this site.
  • No invented numbers. Counts on this site rebuild themselves from the Apify Store, PyPI and GitHub, and the sample tables come from real runs.
  • Built slow, built steady. At most one new tool a day, and only once it works.
